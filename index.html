Waffles

Warm homemade waffles served fresh.

Rating & Conversion

Difficulty Level

Conversion

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 3/4 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder

Equipment

  • Mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • Measuring cups
  • Waffle maker

Directions

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add eggs, milk, and oil.
  3. Stir until combined.
  4. Pour batter into waffle maker.
  5. Cook until golden brown.
